About Yana

Beauty has always been more than skin deep to me.

I'm Yana, a licensed esthetician with over six years of experience in skincare, facial massage, and wellness. My work has taken me through spas, advanced skincare practices, and holistic wellness spaces, where I discovered what I love most: the relationship between the skin, the body, our emotions, and the way we experience ourselves.

I believe that caring for your skin can be a ritual rather than another task on your list. A chance to slow down. To breathe. To soften your face, your body, and perhaps even the way you speak to yourself.

My treatments are designed to leave you with more than glowing skin. I want you to leave feeling present, restored, and a little more at home in yourself.
